Diagnostics

Paints the page magenta, with a cyan band over its last 60 points, on a red backdrop. Read the bottom edge of the screen for six seconds: cyan means the web view genuinely ends where the page ends and those points are unreachable; red means there are pixels below the page that this app simply isn't painting.
